Theoretical physics runs on a particular kind of labour: the derivation checked line by line, the ansatz you guess and then have to earn, the argument you only believe once you have rebuilt it yourself. Systems that can do parts of that work now sit on our desks, next to the notebook and the preprint queue.

What is unsettled is not whether this is happening, but what it means: for what counts as a derivation and who counts as its author, for how physical intuition gets built over the course of a PhD, for which calculations are worth a person’s time at all.

This is a communal blog by and for people working in high-energy theory and quantum gravity — somewhere to think aloud about that shift without first having to justify the premise or flatten the technical stakes. Enthusiasm, indifference, irritation and flat scepticism are all part of the record worth keeping.
