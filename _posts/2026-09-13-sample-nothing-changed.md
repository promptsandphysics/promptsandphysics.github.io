I keep reading that the field is being transformed and I keep not experiencing
it. I am two years into a PhD on non-perturbative aspects of the gauge/gravity
correspondence. I have tried the tools four or five times. Each time they were
impressive for about twenty minutes and then produced something subtly wrong
in a way that took me longer to find than the calculation would have taken me
to do.

I am not making an argument that this will always be true. I have no idea
whether it will be true next year. I only want it recorded somewhere that in
September 2026 there was a PhD student for whom this was, so far, a
non-event — because when the histories get written I suspect that experience
will be edited out, and it was quite common.
